**Top 3 structural ideas for the remaining gap (8 → 2–5):**
1. **More computation per network call, at the same parameter count.** Reuse the same weights more than once inside `forward` (weight-tied recursion), or run the 32-px level at twice the internal resolution. The 1M limit counts parameters, not compute, and our curve says capacity is what binds. *Rule status:* allowed as architecture, since it's still one `forward` call per sampling step. I'd want your ruling on recursion specifically, though, because it is close to "changing the effective number of network evaluations".
2. **Self-conditioning** (Chen et al., "Analog Bits", ICLR 2023): the network also sees its own previous x̂₀ estimate. Training would use two forward passes per update, still one batch and one optimiser step. At sampling, `forward` would have to remember its last x̂₀ between the sampler's calls. *Rule status:* grey on both counts and needs your ruling.
3. **Separate expert networks for different noise levels** (eDiff-I, Balaji et al. 2022): split the budget, for example high-noise and low-noise U-Nets, with `forward` choosing by t. Each sampling step still makes exactly one call, and the 100 DDPM steps make specialising across t worthwhile. *Rule status:* allowed (architecture). The risk is that each expert gets about half the capacity.
