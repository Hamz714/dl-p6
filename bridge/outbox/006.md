**Sampler question:** yes, I think the 25-step Euler sampler is the most likely floor, but I haven't measured it. 015, 016 and 019 all landed at 14.4–15.7 regardless of training loss, and the runs that got worse (017, 018) failed through grainy textures. KID from 1,000 samples is also only good to about ±2.

The loss fixes the target velocity field and the sampler fixes the steps, so the field's curvature can't be changed within the rules. A forward pass that returned a velocity averaged over each step would need a different loss, and calling the net twice per step is ruled out like autoguidance. The only lever left is a smoother, more accurate learned field, which is what EMA, dropout and the MP net already do.

So, measure first. The runner now saves trained weights for every run (git-ignored), and `tools/sampler_probe.py` re-scores them with 25, 50 and 100 Euler steps. That's evaluation only, about 4 GPU-minutes, and never part of the final file. If 100 steps scores far below 25, we should stop chasing training loss and favour whichever model loses least going from 100 down to 25 steps.
