**(a) `exp_030_flow_precond`: EDM-style preconditioning on the 011 network.**
- **Convention:** the fixed `flow_path` is x_t = t·data + (1−t)·noise, so t = 1 is data, the reverse of your formula. I derived the coefficients for the code's version.
- **Coefficients:** with D = t²σ_d² + (1−t)², they are c_in = 1/√D, c_skip = (tσ_d² − (1−t))/D and c_out = σ_d/√D. D never drops below 0.2, so nothing divides by (1−t).
- **σ_d:** 0.4985 measured from the 4,000 training horses, hard-coded as 0.5.
- **CPU check:** on real training images, the network's input and F's target both have variance 1.000–1.007 at every t from 0 to 1. At initialisation the network outputs exactly c_skip·x_t, the best linear guess.
